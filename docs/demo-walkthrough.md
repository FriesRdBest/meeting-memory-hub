# Demo walkthrough: Signal → Action → Reflection

This guide walks a stakeholder through a complete end-to-end flow in the Meeting Memory Console prototype.

## Prerequisites

- You have the application running locally or in a Codespace.
- You are on the `main` branch (which includes the demo banners).

## Step 1 — Signal Desk

1. Open the app and navigate to **Signal Desk**.
2. At the top, you will see a blue info box:  
   > Demo mode: For a quick end-to-end walkthrough, start with **SIG-001** "Repeated onboarding friction is slowing adoption". Create an action for it in Action Queue, then record a reflection in Learning Loop.
3. Review **SIG-001**:
   - Read the evidence.
   - Note the proposed destination and owner.
4. Remember this signal; you will create an action for it next.

## Step 2 — Action Queue

1. Navigate to **Action Queue**.
2. You will see a blue info box:  
   > Demo mode: For the walkthrough, choose **SIG-001** "Repeated onboarding friction…" below, then use "Start work" → "Mark completed". After that, record a reflection in Learning Loop.
3. In **Inspect and decide**, choose:
   - **SIG-001 · Repeated onboarding friction is slowing adoption**
4. In the form:
   - Decision: **Start work**
   - Confirm or adjust owner and destination.
   - Optionally add a decision note.
   - Click **Record decision**.
5. After the page reloads, you can optionally:
   - Change Decision to **Mark completed** and record another decision to simulate finishing the work.

## Step 3 — Learning Loop

1. Navigate to **Learning Loop**.
2. You will see a blue info box:  
   > Demo mode: After completing your action for **SIG-001** in Action Queue, choose it below and record a reflection. This completes the Signal → Action → Reflection demo.
3. In **Record learning from completed work**:
   - Choose the completed action for **SIG-001** from the dropdown.
4. Fill in:
   - **Observed outcome**: what happened after the action was completed.
   - **Learning retained**: what the organization should carry forward.
   - **Next step** (optional): any follow-up work.
5. Click **Save learning record**.

Your new reflection will appear in the list of completed records, linked back to the original signal evidence.

## What this proves

- Signals from conversations can be reviewed, routed, and owned.
- Human decisions are recorded before action is taken.
- Outcomes and learning are preserved as organizational memory.
- The full path from signal → action → reflection is visible and inspectable.
